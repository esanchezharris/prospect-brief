1Signal watch

Who with a stated affiliation to the institution appeared in SEC filings recently? IPO registrations, officer and director appointments, proxy statements. Public EDGAR filings only.

This is where production would match names against the CRM. The demo stops at the list: pick a name and build the brief.

2Build a brief

Name plus at least one identity anchor. The tool resolves identity first, then researches, verifies every claim in code, and writes the brief from verified evidence only.

A real brief takes about 5 minutes and about $1 in model and search calls.

3Trust check

The same pipeline on a fictional person whose corpus contains planted traps: a fabricated quote, a wrong dollar figure, a claim its quote doesn't support, a namesake, a page that disagrees on an amount, a blocked site, a robots.txt-forbidden page, and a hidden "ignore previous instructions". No keys, no network, a few seconds.
